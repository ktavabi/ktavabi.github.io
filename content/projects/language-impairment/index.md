Regulatory-style validation of a speech-discrimination biomarker for language impairment in autism using blinded ROC and mixed models.
<!--more-->

**Role:** Post-Doctoral Researcher, Lurie Family Foundations MEG Imaging Center, CHOP (2008-2011)

**Highlights:**
- AUC 0.86; sensitivity 82.4%; specificity 71.2%; Cohen's d = 3.11
- 78 pediatric participants; 301 observations
- Bonferroni correction; pre-specified criteria; nonparametric linear mixed modeling

**Outcomes:** Roberts et al. (2011). *Biological Psychiatry* -- highly cited clinical biomarker paper

`MEG` `ROC analysis` `linear mixed models` `MATLAB`
