Python pipelines from 306-channel MEG time series to predictive models of language and reading outcomes across pediatric cohorts.
<!--more-->

**Role:** Research Science Engineer, I-LABS, University of Washington (2011-2023)

**Highlights:**
- Infant vocabulary prediction (r = 0.73, p = 0.001)
- Reading-skill biomarker (r = 0.67, p = 1x10^-6)
- Linear mixed effects, cluster permutation testing, dimensionality reduction

**Outcomes:** *Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience* (2021); *Brain and Language* (2021)
